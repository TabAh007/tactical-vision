## 2. Core Services

### 1. Product Development
- **Summary**: Creating market-ready products through ideation, design, engineering, and manufacturing processes for commercial success.
- **Details**: With over 12 years of industry expertise, end-to-end product development from initial napkin sketches and 3D CAD modeling to factory mass production and compliance.

### 2. Industrial Design
- **Summary**: Precision-crafted industrial design embodying aesthetics, ergonomic comfort, usability, and optimal manufacturability (DFM).
- **Details**: Optimizing every curve, texture, material selection, and wall thickness to elevate market appeal.

### 3. Mechanical Engineering
- **Summary**: Mechanical design, structural analysis, tolerance stack-up, production-ready CAD, fluid mechanics, and kinematics.
- **Details**: Turning complex engineering challenges into robust, fail-safe physical components.

### 4. Reverse Engineering
- **Summary**: Meticulous 3D scanning, teardown analysis, and deconstruction of existing mechanical components to replicate, modernize, or enhance performance.

### 5. Product Prototyping
- **Summary**: High-fidelity functional prototypes (SLA/SLS 3D printing, CNC machining, silicone tooling) to test ergonomics, fit, and structural integrity before tooling investment.

### 6. Product Manufacturing & Sourcing
- **Summary**: Seamless progression from CAD to high-volume injection molding, die casting, CNC milling, sheet metal fabrication, assembly, and QA with vetted international factory partners.

### 7. Strategic Consultation & Scoping
- **Summary**: Free initial scoping covering project objectives, timeline & milestones, budget & BOM estimation, and technical constraints.
